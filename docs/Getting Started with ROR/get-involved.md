## ROR discussion groups

Register for one or more of the following open, public forums to ask questions and discuss issues with a wide range of people interested in ROR:

* [ROR Technical Forum](https://groups.google.com/a/ror.org/g/ror-tech): Technical announcements, discussions, and questions for developers implementing ROR in their systems

* [ROR Community Forum](https://groups.google.com/a/ror.org/g/ror-community): General news, event reminders, and discussion about ROR

## ROR community calls

Every other month, ROR hosts an hour-long online meeting to give updates on what's new with ROR, discuss ROR's plans, and invite feedback and answer questions from ROR supporters. Register for upcoming calls on [ROR's events page](https://ror.org/events) .
