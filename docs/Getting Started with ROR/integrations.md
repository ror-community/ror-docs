See ROR IDs in action! A number of systems have already integrated or are working on integrating ROR in order to collect and display clean and consistent affiliation data.

* **Is your integration missing from this list?** [Tell us about it!](https://airtable.com/app2Tnq1uCHnS8HQD/shrzY2wVF5ebL0Fm5) 
* **Is your integration on the list, but in need of updates/corrections?** Email [support@ror.org](mailto:support@ror.org).
* See also the [GitHub list of ROR users](https://github.com/stars/amandafrench/lists/github-list-of-ror-users) for codebases on GitHub that use ROR. 
* Find recordings of webinars and conference presentations on the [ROR YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCQBOpOpW-JEKoVCUlmCK1Eg/playlists).
